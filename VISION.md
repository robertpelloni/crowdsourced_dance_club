# Vision

## The Goal
To create the world's first truly collaborative, AI-driven club experience. Crowd engagement doesn't just influence the music—it steers the entire venue's vibe through a cybernetic feedback loop between the audience, the AI Conductor, and the Real-Time Audio Engine.

## Phase 1: Cybernetic Intelligence (Complete)
- AI-driven track selection and real-time feedback loop.
- C++ Audio Engine with low-latency DSP.
- ML-driven transition prediction (v2.2.0).

## Phase 2: Global Expansion (Active)
- **Decentralized Venues:** Scalable architecture for simultaneous events across different geographical locations. (Multi-tenant venue DB & geo-proximity integrated v2.5.0)
- **Hardware Ecosystem:** Native support for professional lighting (DMX) and high-fidelity audio hardware. (DMX support integrated v2.4.0).
- **Unified Vibe Identity:** Global leaderboards and persistent musical identities for users.
